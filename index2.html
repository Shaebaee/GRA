Jamaican Rum Cake

Perfect cake to get a little buzz on a special occasions. This cake can be prefectly pair with ice cream as well.

Before Baking

Part 1: Prepare the Soaked Fruit (Advance Prep)

Combine dried fruits (common choices include raisins, prunes, currants, and glacé cherries) in an airtight container. Cover the fruit completely with dark rum and sweet wine (like port or red label wine). Soak the fruit for a minimum of 24 hours, but ideally for several weeks or months for optimal flavor development. On baking day, blend the soaked fruits into a coarse paste using a food processor.

Ingredients

  • Salted Butter
  • Dark Brown Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Browning
  • Molasses
  • Ground Cinnamon
  • Ground Nutmeg
  • Ground Allspice
  • All Purpose Flour
  • Baking Powder
  • Salt
  • Fruit (Raisins)
  • Wray Nephew Overproof Rum or Red Label Wine
  • Evaporated Milk or Heavy Cream

Instructions

Part 2: Prepare and Bake the Cake

  • Preheat the oven to 300°F–325°F (150°C–165°C).
  • Cream butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
  • Add eggs one at a time with vanilla and lime zest.
  • Mix in fruit paste, browning sauce, and molasses.
  • Sift dry ingredients in a separate bowl.
  • Gently fold dry into wet ingredients.
  • Pour batter into greased pan(s).
  • Bake 1.5–2.5 hours until toothpick comes out clean.
