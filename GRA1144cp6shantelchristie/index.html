About the brand

Hot Gyal Brand is all about confidence, femininity, and standing out while being classy and sexy at the same time. Created by CEO Shantel Christie, the brand was born from a personal journey of rebuilding self-esteem after weight gain caused by PCOS. She wanted to create a brand that makes women feel beautiful, confident, and comfortable in whatever they wear.
