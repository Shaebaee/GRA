Jamaican Rum Cake

Ingredients

    Salted Butter, Dark Brown Sugar, Eggs, Vanilla Extract, Browning, Molassaes, Ground Cinnamon, Ground Nutmeg and Ground Allspice, All purpose Flour, Baking Powder, Salt, Fruit (Raisin), Wray and Nephew Overproof Rum, J.Wrayand Nephew Red Label, Evaporated milk or Heavy cream.

Instructions

Before Baking

Part 1: Prepare the Soaked Fruit (Advance Prep)

*Combine dried fruits (common choices include raisins, prunes, currants, and glacé cherries) in an airtight container. Cover the fruit completely with dark rum and sweet wine (like port or red label wine). Soak the fruit for a minimum of 24 hours, but ideally for several weeks or months for optimal flavor development. On baking day, blend the soaked fruits into a coarse paste using a food processor.*

Part 2: Prepare and Bake the Cake

  • Preheat the oven to a low temperature, typically around 300°F to 325°F (150°C to 165°C), to ensure slow, even baking and prevent drying.
  • Cream butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
  • Add eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition, along with flavorings like vanilla extract and lime zest.
  • Mix in the blended fruit paste, browning sauce, and molasses.
  • Sift together dry ingredients (flour, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, allspice, and salt) in a separate bowl.
  • Gently fold the dry mixture into the wet ingredients using a cut and fold method until just combined; avoid overmixing.
  • Pour the batter into a greased and lined cake pan(s).
  • Bake for approximately 1.5 to 2.5 hours, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean. Placing a pan of water in the oven can help keep the cake moist.
